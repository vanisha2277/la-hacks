# P&ID Senior Engineer

Ask about the flash unit's P&IDs the way you'd ask the senior engineer who owned them: equipment, control loops, relief protection, history of changes, and known gaps.

**It never tells you anything it can't show you.** Every plant fact is cited to a drawing, Slack message, Drive document, instrument index row, or senior note, and every answer lists the tool calls it made ("How I found this"). If something isn't recorded, it says so.

Example questions:
- Why does V-200 have two level transmitters?
- What protects the flash drum from overpressure?
- Is the preheater bypass still in service?
- What gaps are there on PID-102?
